A search over interaction laws

Most rules make static. Some do not.

AXIOM is a library. A rule is a flat list of floats; the engine turns one into a running Particle-Lenia simulation, the tuner reduces that simulation to a bounded row of numbers and judges it, and the search breeds the rules whose numbers came back interesting. Nothing here is sampled from a trained model, and nothing here is drawn.

PART I

The object

What is actually in the repository, how it is split, and what a candidate looks like.

What the crate is

One Rust library with four modules and no binaries. It does not open a file, print anything, or draw anything. A search is assembled in code, run inside the calling process, and handed back as a ranked list of survivors that exists for exactly as long as the caller holds it.

AnalogyA wind tunnel, not an aircraft›

This is equipment for finding out whether a shape flies. You bolt a candidate in, run the fan, and read the instruments. It does not take off, it does not carry passengers, and it has no cockpit.

Everything the repository contains is the tunnel and the instruments: a simulator, a set of gauges, a rule for rejecting a candidate mid-run, and a breeding loop that proposes the next batch of shapes.

TechnicalFour modules›

engine is a simulation as a pure function of a genome. run takes one genome in and hands one descriptor out. tuner measures, gates, scores, and searches. util holds the seeded xorshift, Euclidean distance, and the non-finite guard.

A caller builds a Search, calls run, and gets a Vec<Specimen> sorted best score first. Each Specimen carries its genome, its descriptor, and its score. Nothing is serialized, so a search cannot be stopped and continued.

Two halves and a hinge

The simulator and the search never speak to each other directly. The simulator does not know it is being measured, and the search never builds a simulation. One small module in the middle owns that meeting, which is the only place the two touch.

ModuleMay not
engineimport from tuner or run
tuneropen a file, print, or build a simulation
rundecide what to measure; it decides only when to look
AnalogyThe patient does not read the chart›

A drug trial goes wrong the moment the treatment knows which measurements are being taken, because it starts optimizing for the measurement rather than the outcome. Keeping the two apart is what makes the reading mean anything.

Here it is enforced by the import graph rather than by discipline. The simulator cannot consult the scoring function even by accident, because it cannot see it.

One flat vector

Everything a search can change about a rule lives in a single flat list of floats. The mutation operators treat it as an anonymous vector and never need to know what any one number means. The shape of that list, how many anchors and how many mixture components, rides alongside in a second structure the search holds still.

[ coordination ]
[ anchor_count trait logits ]
[ anchor_count² pair blocks, source-major ]

pair block =
  shells × [amp, peak, width]   // sensed over distance
  bumps  × [amp, peak, width]   // response over sensed density
  [directed weight]
TechnicalWhy the bounds are taken at the widest box›

Legal ranges for the pair genes depend on the box size, which depends on the coordination gene, which is itself a gene. Bounds are therefore taken at maximum coordination, where the box and the usable kernel reach are widest, so any genome a search can produce stays inside them.

A rollout then re-clamps the pair genes at the box its own coordination gene actually resolves to, where reach is tighter. Clamping replaces a non-finite gene with its lower bound, so one NaN cannot poison a decode instead of quietly propagating.

PART II

The simulation

What a particle is, how particles decide to move, and where the box gets its size.

Particles with a trait

Every particle carries a position and one number between zero and one, its trait. The trait is like a species identity, except continuous: a particle can sit halfway between two kinds. Traits are assigned when the population is seeded and never change. Position is the only thing that moves.

On dimensionality

How many spatial axes a particle has is data, read from the parameter set. The substrate, the step, the distance function, and the density probe all take their shape from it. One thing does pin it down: the measurement grid indexes a cubic three-axis lattice, so a search refuses a genome shaped any other way. That constraint belongs to one metric block, not to the physics.

AnalogyA dial, not a label›

Instead of sorting particles into a fixed number of species boxes, give each one a dial reading between 0 and 1. Two particles reading 0.31 and 0.34 are nearly the same kind. One reading 0.9 is something else entirely.

The system places a few anchor points around the dial and lets each particle belong partly to the two it sits between. Sitting exactly on an anchor means belonging wholly to it, which is what makes this an exact generalization of a discrete-species model rather than a replacement for it.

TechnicalHats on a circle, and exact seeding›

Anchors sit evenly spaced on a circle, so the trait axis wraps at the 0/1 seam instead of clamping. Memberships satisfy alpha_a(c) >= 0 and sum_a alpha_a(c) = 1, with at most two adjacent anchors nonzero and exactly one-hot at an anchor.

Seeding matches the population to the genome's logits exactly rather than sampling toward them. Each bin's exact share is floored to an integer, leftover particles go to the bins with the largest fractional remainder with ties broken by index, and each quota is filled with traits drawn uniformly inside that bin's slice.

Every ordered pair, its own law

For each ordered pair of anchors there is a separate rule: how strongly one kind senses another at a given distance, how it responds to feeling that much company, and how hard it acts on the result. Because the pairs are ordered, one kind can chase another that ignores it back.

AnalogyA seating chart with grudges›

Think of a dinner party where every guest has an opinion about every other kind of guest, and the opinions are not mutual. Some want to sit close, some want a specific distance, some want to be near a crowd of one kind and away from another.

The shells are how far away you notice someone. The bumps are your preferred crowding, so being too alone and being too packed both push you to move. The weight is how much you care. Every ordered pair gets its own set of all three.

TechnicalMixtures, truncation, and measured norms›

Both mixtures are Gaussian, and one function returns value and slope in the same loop so the gradient reuses each term. A zero amplitude disables a component, which lets a search learn the active count instead of being told it.

Reach stays below 0.375 of the box so a shell cannot wrap into itself. Each shell truncates at peak + 3.5 × width, roughly 0.2 percent of its peak, and terms with negligible amplitude are skipped so one inert term cannot inflate the radius and erase the saving. This is an explicit approximation: the executed force carries a small hard cutoff there.

Each pair's normalizing constant is measured, not assumed, on a fixed uniform reference population seeded with uniform anchor logits. Seeding that reference with zeroed traits instead would leave every pair except (0, 0) with no mass, silently taking a fallback of 1.0. A test asserts off-diagonal pairs get real measured values.

What one step does

For every particle, walk its neighbors, and for each anchor pair involved accumulate two things: how much company it is sensing, and which direction that company is coming from. Then turn the sensed amount into a push along that direction, take one small step, and wrap back into the box.

// per neighbor: at most four pairs are live, because each side has at most two anchors
potential[pair] += source_weight × K(d)
gradient[pair]  += source_weight × K'(d) / d × direction

// then, per pair: convert sensed density into motion
step += 2 × dt × receiver_weight × weight × B'(potential / norm) / norm × gradient
TechnicalThe factor of two, and the two loops›

The 2 is the surviving term of G = 2B - 1 under differentiation. Only the slope enters movement, so the -1 dies and the factor stays.

Accumulation runs only over pairs where both memberships are nonzero, at most four per neighbor. Conversion then runs over every possible source against the receiver's active anchors, because a source anchor with no local mass still has a defined response at zero sensed density.

Distance is minimum-image with softening added last, after the per-axis squares, matching the accumulation order callers depend on. The square root is taken lazily, at most once per neighbor, and skipped entirely when the squared distance is already past the pair's squared reach. The integration is explicit Euler, wrapped with rem_euclid.

Density decides the box

The size of the box is not a free choice. The genome asks for a target number of neighbors per particle, and the box size follows from that plus the particle count and the interaction radius. Doubling the particles at the same crowding gives a bigger box with the same local texture rather than a denser one.

TechnicalOne probe, measured once, reused›

The probe measures the mean potential a particle senses in a uniform population at a trial box of six radii, using a shell shaped by radius alone rather than any one genome's actual law, so every candidate in a search measures against the same reference. It caps at 1,500 particles and rescales by the true density ratio, which bounds the O(n²) cost.

A genome's coordination gene then rescales that reference by the dimensional root. Because the probe depends only on particle count, dimensionality, and radius, and none of those vary per run, a search builds one and threads it through every candidate rather than re-measuring.

PART III

Measuring

How a running simulation becomes a row of numbers, and what those numbers had to survive to be trusted.

Nothing measures everything

There is no fixed list of measurements taken every time. The scoring function names what it scores on, the rejection rules name what they judge, and the union of those two, plus whatever they depend on, is exactly what gets computed. Everything else is not paid for.

AnalogyThe lab only runs the tests you ordered›

A blood panel is not one fixed sheet of every assay that exists. You order what the question needs, the lab adds anything an ordered test depends on, and you are billed for that set.

The consequence worth knowing: a descriptor is a bare row of numbers with no labels on it. Reading one entry needs the row and the order sheet that produced it. Hand it the wrong sheet and you read the wrong number.

TechnicalClosing the set in one pass›

Each metric is a &'static Spec written in its own file, declaring a stable key, its width in slots, which grids it reads, which metrics it depends on, how it reduces across samples, and which shared blocks it needs.

ALL lists all ten in dependency order. plan walks it backwards once to close the wanted set over dependencies, then filters ALL, which yields dependencies ahead of dependents without ever sorting. A metric the plan skipped, and a descriptor from a simulation that died, both read empty rather than reading zero and looking like a measurement.

Fifty-four numbers

When every metric runs, a simulation reduces to fifty-four values. Each one is squashed into the range zero to one, so no single raw statistic with a naturally wide range can dominate a comparison between two rules.

asymmetry is the odd one out: every other metric reads the simulation, and that one reads the law that produced it, so a search can tell a mutual attraction from a chase.

AnalogyTwo corrections that stop the numbers lying›

Scatter rice uniformly on a tiled floor and count grains per tile. On big tiles the counts look even. On small tiles some tiles get three grains and some get none, and a naive reading calls that structure. It is not structure, it is the arithmetic of small samples.

The heterogeneity metric subtracts exactly what randomness would have contributed, twice: once for the density counts, once for the trait mixtures inside each cell. Without those subtractions a uniformly mixed population reads as though it had grown distinct regions, purely because the grid was fine.

TechnicalWinding, spanning forests, and reading yourself›

Connectivity blurs density with a separable three-tap kernel before any threshold, so one stray particle cannot register as a phase. It then flood-fills carrying an integer lift, the count of steps taken along each axis to reach a cell without wrapping. Arriving at a visited cell with a disagreeing lift means the two walks differ by a full loop, which detects a box-spanning phase using nothing but integers. A phase counts only if it fills at least 8 percent of the grid and one component carries such a loop, so a compact blob is disconnected here however many of its cells touch.

Topology uses the fact that H0 death scales are exactly the edge weights of the Euclidean minimum spanning tree, so the whole cluster hierarchy costs one spanning forest. Edges stop at the kernel cutoff, deliberately: clumps no kernel can reach across are not one structure at any scale.

Temporal is the only metric that reads other metrics. It pulls three slices out of each sampled descriptor, lays them end to end as that tick's picture, and asks how far the picture moved and how much it still resembles itself a few samples later. A lag correlation near 1 means it looks the same later, so a frozen crystal and a slow drift both read high. With too few samples it assumes static, which fails a variance floor rather than sneaking past one.

Paying once for shared work

Several metrics want the same expensive intermediate: the same grid, the same table of pairwise distances, the same neighbor graph. Those are built once per sampled moment, from the union of what was ordered, and handed to everyone who asked. No metric builds its own.

Asking for what you did not order

Reaching for a block the plan never requested panics. A metric that quietly returned zero instead would produce a plausible number that means nothing, and a search would happily optimize it.

TechnicalTwo details that are not decoration›

Grid sides are deduplicated before building, so two metrics wanting one resolution cost one grid.

The pair histogram accumulates in f64. A thousand-particle simulation contributes half a million pairs, and f32 starts dropping counts at that magnitude. The pair walk is deliberately all-pairs rather than grid-accelerated, because its far bins reach the box half-diagonal, well past any kernel cutoff the neighbor grid could exploit.

The metric that runs an experiment

Nine of the ten metrics watch. One intervenes. It copies the simulation, shakes one neighborhood of particles out of place, runs both the damaged copy and an untouched control forward, and asks how far the damaged one got back toward where the control ended up.

AnalogyCompare against where it was going, not where it was›

To test whether a wound heals you do not compare the patient against a photograph taken before the injury. Everyone changes over an hour, injured or not. You compare against what the same patient would have looked like an hour later, uninjured.

That is why an untouched control is run forward alongside the damaged copy. Comparing against the present state instead would bill ordinary drift as unhealed damage, and every rule that simply moves would score as fragile.

TechnicalA share, not a count, and no recursion›

The score is a mean share of damage closed rather than a count of probes clearing a threshold. Counting gave a step function with four levels that sat on zero for most genomes, so anything multiplying by it lost its gradient entirely. A probe whose shake moved the signature by less than 0.01 is discarded as proving nothing; a copy that blows up closes nothing and is scored as such.

Damage is measured in the same space the search already thinks in: the signature is three other metrics, read through the same plan machinery the descriptor uses. Robustness is deliberately absent from that list, which is what keeps it from recursing into itself.

Its four constants are fixed rather than run settings. Changing any of them changes what a robustness reading means, so two runs that disagreed about them were never comparable in the first place.

PART IV

Judging and searching

When to look, what disqualifies a candidate, what counts as good, and how the next batch is proposed.

When to look

A rollout does not measure continuously. It steps, and stops to look eight times, spread across the second half of the run. Anything that has blown up is abandoned on the spot, and anything failing its checks twice in a row is abandoned before it finishes.

  1. Decode and resolve.

    Split the flat vector, derive the box from the coordination gene, re-clamp the pair genes at that box.

  2. Take a baseline.

    The uniform starting cloud is the reference every structural reading compares against.

  3. Step and sample.

    Eight samples at a fixed interval across the latter half. A non-finite coordinate ends it immediately.

  4. Check as you go.

    From the second sample on. Two consecutive failures end the rollout early.

  5. Fold.

    Averaged metrics average, history-reading metrics take the last sample.

Why patience is two, not one

A simulation can dip out of a band and climb back within a single sample. Abandoning on the first failure would throw away rules that recover, which are exactly the interesting ones. An abandoned rollout returns an empty descriptor, which reads zero everywhere and so clears no check that carries a floor.

A range, not a score

A gate is a floor and a ceiling on one measurement, read in that measurement's own units. It answers whether a candidate is worth ranking at all. It never ranks. Gates are supplied by the caller when the search is built rather than baked in as constants.

AnalogyA height limit, not a beauty contest›

The sign at the ride entrance does not rank riders. It admits or refuses, and being far above the line buys nothing. Judging quality is a separate job done by someone else, on the people who got in.

Keeping the two separate is why no scoring function in this codebase reads a threshold, and why no gate returns a number.

TechnicalPartial readings›

A check taken mid-rollout skips any gate whose metric only lands on the final sample, since that metric has not been measured yet and its slots are still zero. The final check judges everything.

A dead simulation reads zero across the board, so any gate carrying a floor rejects it without needing a special case for death.

Two ways to want something

A scoring function produces one number and names what it needs measured. There are two, and they want opposite things: one rewards being unlike everything found so far, the other rewards hitting a specific description of interesting.

Novelty
  • Mean distance to the fifteen nearest behaviors already found.
  • Reads the whole descriptor, so it needs no layout.
  • No target, so it dodges the trivial attractors an absolute score falls into.
  • And equally: it never climbs toward a goal you had in mind.
Structure
  • Five clauses: contrast, connected material, connected void, repair, turnover.
  • Absolute, so it can be climbed toward.
  • And equally: it can be fooled. A static lattice satisfies the contrast term outright.
  • Clauses multiply, so weakness anywhere costs everywhere.
AnalogyThe floor under the product›

Multiplying five requirements together says all five must hold, which is what you want. But multiplied raw, one zero makes the whole thing zero, and then a candidate scoring zero on one clause is indistinguishable from a candidate scoring zero on all five.

That is not a hypothetical. Nearly every candidate scored exactly zero, the tie-break in the parent tournament fell to whichever was drawn first, and the search quietly degraded into random search while still reporting scores. Flooring each clause at 0.02 keeps the conjunction expensive, an order of magnitude, while leaving the other four rankable.

TechnicalWhy the population is re-scored every generation›

Novelty is population-relative, so every stored score goes stale the moment the population moves around it. The whole population is re-scored before anything is ranked or selected from. Each member is scored against a set that contains it, which biases everyone identically and therefore cannot change an ordering.

Novelty's list of metrics is not a dependency list. Novelty names no metric of its own; it wants a space to be far apart in, and the gates widen that space too, since distance reads the whole plan.

Three lanes and a way out

Each generation proposes a batch of candidates from three sources: mutations of what is scoring well, mutations of whatever sits nearest a randomly chosen point in behavior space, and completely fresh draws. Fresh draws take whatever the first two do not claim, so a converged population always has an exit.

child = a
      + iso  × gene_range × Normal(0, I)   // per-gene noise, scaled to each gene's own range
      + line × Normal(0, 1) × (b - a)      // one shared step toward a second parent
AnalogyThe baseline that has to be beaten›

Zero out both named lanes and every candidate comes back a fresh uniform draw. The whole apparatus collapses into random search, on purpose, because that is the control condition.

If random matches evolution under some scoring function, that scoring function is not steering, and no amount of generations will fix it. Having the baseline reachable by setting two numbers to zero makes it cheap to check.

TechnicalExpeditions, and why a converged batch cannot take over›

An expedition throws a dart at a uniform point in descriptor space and mutates whichever member landed nearest it. In dozens of dimensions most uniform targets sit far from anything realized, so this is honestly a random goal direction rather than coverage-aware illumination. That is the intent: distant intentions without a model inventing state.

Retention sorts best score first and keeps a member only if it sits clear of everything already kept. The cutoff is a quarter of the population's own median nearest-neighbor spacing, a fraction rather than an absolute distance, so it holds whatever units and however many axes a descriptor turns out to have. A population of identical genomes has zero spacing and drops nothing, which is what lets an opening generation through.

Batches evaluate in parallel through an order-preserving filter, so a parallel batch judges identically to a serial one.

Where the pieces live, and what is honestly absent.

What the code does not contain

Read these as absences in the repository rather than as a roadmap. Some are deliberate scope, some are unfinished, and the document does not pretend to know which is which.

AbsentConsequence
Trait dynamicsTraits are frozen for a rollout. No birth, death, or inheritance.
SerializationNothing reaches disk, so a population dies with its process and a search cannot be continued.
An entry pointNo binary, no command line, and nothing that draws.
A second search familyOne algorithm, so no claim that it beats anything is supported here.
Dimension-general measurementThe grid pins a search to three axes even though the engine does not.
A stable integratorExplicit Euler; divergence is caught after the fact, not prevented.
An exact forceThe shell truncation puts a small hard cutoff in what is actually integrated.
A learned descriptorThe metric set is hand-designed and encodes a prior about what is worth noticing.

The structure criterion can be satisfied on its contrast term by a static lattice. Only turnover and repair stand against that, and both are floored rather than required.

What the tests do and do not establish

Twenty tests cover code-level behavior: memberships, seeding, the two neighbor walks agreeing, bit-reproducibility, plan ordering, gate rejection, the once-per-rollout schedule, and that the structure criterion can tell genomes apart. None of them is evidence that the search finds anything worth finding.

AXIOM

A library that searches Particle-Lenia interaction laws. The engine simulates a genome, the tuner reduces the result to a bounded descriptor and judges it, and the search breeds what scored well. Nothing here is drawn, stored, or sampled from a trained model.
